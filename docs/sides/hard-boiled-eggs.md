## Cooking

1. Pierce the bottom of the eggs with a pin.
2. Place eggs in pot, and cover with cool water by about 1 inch.
3. Bring water to boil over medium heat.
4. Remove from heat and cover with lid, let sit for 12 minutes.
5. Move eggs to cold water to stop the cooking.
6. Unpealed eggs will keep in the fridge for quite a while.

## Notes

- Eggs that are a week to ten days old before cooking are easier to peal.
