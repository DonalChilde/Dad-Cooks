# Butter Sandwich Bread

A nice white bread base with oatmeal for some texture.

| Overview   |            |
| :--------- | :--------- |
| Source     | Chad       |
| Source Url |            |
| Prep Time  | 4 hours    |
| Cook Time  | 45 minutes |
| Servings   | 2 loaves   |

## Ingredients

|  Qty | Units | Name                      |
| ---: | :---- | :------------------------ |
| 1020 | grams | Unbleached Bread Flour    |
|  170 | grams | Rolled Oats               |
|  710 | grams | Warm Water (~110 degrees) |
|  1.5 | tbsp  | Kosher Salt               |
|  1.5 | tbsp  | Brown Sugar               |
|  1.5 | tbsp  | Instant Yeast             |
|    4 | tbsp  | Salted Butter             |

## Equipment

- 2 Medium loaf pans
- Instant read thermometer
- kitchen scale

## Preparation

1. Place dry ingredients into large bowl, mix well.
1. Pour warm water into bowl, while mixing evenly. Dough should end up as a slightly wet ball. Do not Kneed.
1. Lightly grease a large bowl with butter.
1. Place dough in bowl and cover - allow to rise. Dough should double in size, about 1-2 hours. You can use greased wax paper and a dish towel to cover bowl. Bowl should be placed in a warm spot. e.g. A sunny spot by the window. If its cold in the house, you can place the bowl on top of a heating pad.
1. Lightly coat two bread pans with butter.
1. Divide dough into 2 evenly sized balls.
1. Dust a cutting board with flour, and work the dough - Flattening and folding at least ten times.
1. Shape dough into a loaf, tucking the folds underneath, and place in greased pan. Try to make the loaf evenly shaped.
1. Lightly bush top of dough with butter. Score top of loaf if desired. Both of these steps aid rising.
1. Repeat
1. Cover pans with greased wax paper and a dish towel. Allow to rise until dough crowns over the top of the pan, about 2 hours.
1. Turn out bread on a cooling rack. Allow bread to cool before cutting.

## Cooking

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
1. Place an empty pan on the bottom rack.
1. After bread has risen, Lightly brush tops with melted butter.
1. Place loaf pans on middle rack.
1. Pour a cup of watter into empty pan on lower rack.
1. Cook at 350 for 45 minutes. Bread should reach an internal temp of 190 degrees.

## Notes

- Substitute honey or maple syrup for brown sugar.
- Add nuts or other mix ins during dough working step.
- Do not use whole wheat flour for this recipie.
