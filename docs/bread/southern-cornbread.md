# Southern Cornbread

Cornbread as it was meant to be.

## Ingredients

| Qty | Units | Name            |
| --: | :---- | :-------------- |
|   2 | cups  | Yellow Cornmeal |
|   2 | tsp   | Sugar           |
|   2 | tsp   | Baking Powder   |
| 1/2 | tsp   | Salt            |
| 1/2 | tsp   | Baking Soda     |
| 2/3 | cup   | Boiling Water   |
|   1 | cup   | Whole Milk      |
| 1/2 | cup   | Greek Yogurt    |
|   2 | Large | Eggs            |
|   1 | stick | Salted Butter   |
|   1 | tbsp  | Bacon Grease    |

## Equipment

A well cured cast iron skillet. At least 8-9 inches. Wider makes a thinner cornbread.

You could also use a cast iron griddle to make cornbread pancakes.

## Cooking

1. Pre-heat oven to 450 degrees. Put bacon grease in the skillet, and put the skillet in the oven to pre-heat.
1. Measure 2/3 cup of cornmeal into a medium bowl.
1. Mix remaining dry ingredients into a small bowl.
1. Pour boiling water into medium bowl, stir into a stiff mush.
1. Melt the butter (not too hot), and mix in the eggs. This will help the butter mix evenly into the batter.
1. Mix the milk, yogurt, and egg-butter mixture together.
1. Pour a small amount into the medium bowl with the cornmeal mush, and mix thoroughly, Breaking up chunks.
1. Pour remaining liquids into the medium bowl, mix.
1. **When the oven is done preheating**, pour remaining dry ingredients from the small bowl into the medium bowl and mix.
1. Remove hot skillet from oven, and make sure the bacon grease has spread evenly over the bottom.
1. Pour batter into hot skillet. **BEWARE SPLASHING GREASE**
1. Bake until golden brown on top, about 25-27 minutes.
1. Turn out cornbread on cooling rack to maintain maximum cruchy.

## Notes

- The Greek Yogurt is a substitute for buttermilk, which is not available year around where I live. You can also use all whole milk instead, so the total milk measure would be 1 1/2 cups.
- One stick of butter is 8 tbsp, or 1/2 cup.
