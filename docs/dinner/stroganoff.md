## Ingredients

|   Qty | Units  | Name                  |
| ----: | :----- | :-------------------- |
|     2 | tbsp   | Salted Butter         |
|     1 | cup    | Chopped Onions        |
|     2 | cloves | Finely Chopped Garlic |
|     1 | pound  | Ground Turkey         |
| 1 1/2 | cups   | Chicken Broth         |
|     1 | tbsp   | Worchestershire Sauce |
|   3/4 | tsp    | Salt                  |
|   1/4 | tsp    | Pepper                |
|   1/4 | cup    | All Purpose Flour     |
|     1 | cup    | Sour Cream            |
|     6 | cups   | Cooked Egg Noodles    |

## Cooking

1. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat.
1. Cook onions and garlic in butter until tender, about 5-7 minutes. Remove to small bowl.
1. Increase heat to medium-high, cook ground turkey until no longer pink, about 5-7 minutes. **Drain**
1. Stir in 1 cup of broth, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper. Heat to boiling.
1. Beat remaining broth and flour with whisk until incorporated, stir into turkey mixture.
1. Add cooked onions and garlic, return to boiling, and stir until mixture thickens - about 1 minute.
1. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream.
1. Serve with egg noodles.

## Notes

Substitute rice for noodles. Use with or without sour cream.
