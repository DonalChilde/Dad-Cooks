## Ingredients

| Qty | Units  | Name                          |
| --: | :----- | :---------------------------- |
|   4 | lbs.   | Russet or Yukon Gold Potatoes |
|   3 | cloves | Garlic                        |
|   1 | stick  | Salted Butter - melted        |
|   1 | cup    | Milk                          |
|   - | -      | Salt - to taste               |
|   - | -      | Black Pepper - to taste       |

## Equipment

- A large pot
- A hand masher / potato ricer

## Preparation

- Peal and quarter potatoes
- Clean garlic cloves

## Cooking

1. Place potatoes and garlic in a large pot of cold water.
2. Bring pot to boil, and cook for about 15 minutes, until fork tender. Drain well.
3. Heat milk in separate container until warm.
4. Add butter to potatos and begin mashing. Pour in warm milk a little at a time while mashing until desired consistency is reached.
5. Add salt and pepper to taste, serve hot.

## Notes

- Drain Well!
- Hand mashing generally provides better results than mixers.
